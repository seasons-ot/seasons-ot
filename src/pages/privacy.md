_Last updated_: August 18, 2026

Seasons Occupational Therapy (“Seasons OT”, “we”, “us”) provides pediatric occupational therapy to families in the Greater Vancouver region. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you use [seasonsot.com](https://seasonsot.com).

## Information we collect

We may collect:

- **Contact details** you send through our website form, including your name, email address, and message.
- **Waitlist and intake information** if you register through our intake form hosted by Therabyte.
- **Service-related information** you share so we can respond to questions or provide occupational therapy, such as details about your child and family.
- **Basic technical data** that your browser may send automatically, such as pages visited and device type.

We do not require an account to browse this website.

## How we use information

We use personal information to:

- Answer inquiries and follow up on messages sent through the contact form
- Manage waitlist, intake, scheduling, billing, and funding-contract requests
- Provide occupational therapy assessment, treatment, and parent coaching
- Operate and improve this website

We do not sell personal information.

## Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the practice, including:

- **Therabyte**, which hosts our intake and waitlist form
- Service providers who help us operate email, hosting, or clinical records
- Funding or referring partners when you ask us to, or when we are required to
- Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety

## Children’s information

Seasons OT works with children and youth. Information about a child is collected from a parent or guardian. Please do not have a child submit the contact form on their own.

## Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the reasons above, for clinical and legal record-keeping, or as required by professional and legal obligations in British Columbia.

## Your choices

You may email [hello@seasonsot.com](mailto:hello@seasonsot.com) to ask what information we hold, to request a correction, or to ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.
